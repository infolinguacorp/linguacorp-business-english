# 🕐 Sesión 1.3: Información personal — El verbo TO BE

## 📋 El verbo TO BE

| Sujeto | Afirmativo | Negativo | Pregunta |
|--------|-----------|----------|----------|
| I | I **am** | I **am not** | **Am** I...? |
| You | You **are** | You **are not** | **Are** you...? |
| He | He **is** | He **is not** | **Is** he...? |
| She | She **is** | She **is not** | **Is** she...? |
| It | It **is** | It **is not** | **Is** it...? |
| We | We **are** | We **are not** | **Are** we...? |
| They | They **are** | They **are not** | **Are** they...? |

### Contracciones (muy usadas en inglés hablado)

| Completo | Contracción |
|----------|-------------|
| I am | I'm |
| You are | You're |
| He is | He's |
| She is | She's |
| It is | It's |
| We are | We're |
| They are | They're |
| is not | isn't |
| are not | aren't |
| am not | am not (no tiene contracción) |

## 📝 Ejercicio 1: Completa con am, is o are

1. I ____________ a student.
2. She ____________ from Spain.
3. They ____________ engineers.
4. It ____________ a big company.
5. We ____________ in a meeting.
6. You ____________ late.
7. He ____________ the manager.
8. I ____________ not tired.

<details>
<summary>✅ Ver respuestas</summary>

1. **am**
2. **is**
3. **are**
4. **is**
5. **are**
6. **are**
7. **is**
8. **am**

</details>

## 📝 Ejercicio 2: Convierte a preguntas

**Ejemplo:** She is a manager. → Is she a manager?

1. You are from Colombia. → ____________
2. He is in the office. → ____________
3. They are new employees. → ____________
4. It is a difficult project. → ____________
5. We are ready. → ____________

<details>
<summary>✅ Ver respuestas</summary>

1. Are you from Colombia?
2. Is he in the office?
3. Are they new employees?
4. Is it a difficult project?
5. Are we ready?

</details>

## 📝 Ejercicio 3: Convierte a negativo

**Ejemplo:** I am busy. → I am not busy.

1. She is the CEO. → ____________
2. They are in London. → ____________
3. It is expensive. → ____________
4. We are late. → ____________
5. He is angry. → ____________

<details>
<summary>✅ Ver respuestas</summary>

1. She is not / isn't the CEO.
2. They are not / aren't in London.
3. It is not / isn't expensive.
4. We are not / aren't late.
5. He is not / isn't angry.

</details>
