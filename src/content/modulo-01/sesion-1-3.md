# 1.3 El verbo TO BE

## 🎯 Objetivo de la sesión

Dominar el verbo **TO BE** (ser/estar) en sus tres formas del presente: **am, is, are**.

---

## 📚 Gramática: TO BE

### Formas del presente

| Sujeto | Forma | Ejemplo |
|--------|-------|---------|
| I | **am** | I **am** a manager. |
| You / We / They | **are** | You **are** late. |
| He / She / It | **is** | She **is** the CEO. |

### Contracciones (más naturales)

| Completo | Contracción |
|----------|-------------|
| I am | I am |
| You are | You are |
| He is | He is |
| She is | She is |
| It is | It is |
| We are | We are |
| They are | They are |

### Negación

| Afirmativo | Negativo |
|------------|----------|
| I am a student. | I **am not** a student. |
| She is here. | She **is not** here. |
| They are busy. | They **are not** busy. |

### Preguntas

| Pregunta | Respuesta corta |
|----------|-----------------|
| Are you the manager? | Yes, I **am**. / No, I am **not**. |
| Is he from Colombia? | Yes, he **is**. / No, he **is not**. |
| Are they in the meeting? | Yes, they **are**. / No, they **are not**. |

---

## 🎭 Diálogo modelo

::: tip 💼 Escenario: En la oficina

**Receptionist:** Good morning. Are you Mr. Smith?

**Visitor:** Yes, I am. I am here for the 10 o clock meeting.

**Receptionist:** He is in conference room B. She is the project manager. They are waiting for you.

**Visitor:** Thank you. Is the room on the second floor?

**Receptionist:** Yes, it is. The elevator is right there.
:::

---

## 🎙️ Ejercicio de Speaking

Lee en voz alto estas frases:

1. "I **am** the new assistant."
2. "She **is** from Brazil."
3. "They **are** in a meeting."
4. "It **is** my first day."

## 📝 Resumen rápido

::: tip ✅ Recuerda
- **I** → **am**
- **He/She/It** → **is**
- **You/We/They** → **are**
- Negación: **not** después del verbo
- Pregunta: verbo al principio
:::
